# Business Research Assistant

Welcome! I'm a multi-agent AI system that researches companies using real-time web search.

## What I can do

- Research any public or private company
- Summarise recent news, financials, leadership, and strategy
- Answer follow-up questions in context ("What about their competitors?")
- Ask for clarification when your query is too vague

## Example queries

- "Tell me about Tesla"
- "What are Apple's latest earnings?"
- "Who is the CEO of Microsoft and what's their strategy?"
- "What's been happening with OpenAI recently?"
